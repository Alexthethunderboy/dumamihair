Before this goes live

Good bookings begin with clear answers.

Are prices fixed or do they change by length, size and hair?

We need exact pricing rules before the website can calculate a 20% deposit.

Where do appointments happen?

We need the Romford setup, travel area, travel fees and any postcode restrictions.

What is the cancellation and rescheduling policy?

The policy must explain fairly when a deposit moves, is refunded or may be retained.

What does the customer need to do before arriving?

Preparation, hair requirements and late-arrival rules should be clear before payment.
